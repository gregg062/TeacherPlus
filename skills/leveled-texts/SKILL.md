# Leveled texts

## Ask once (only what's missing)

The source text (or the topic, if writing an original passage), grade, and how many levels (default 3: **below grade / on grade / above grade**). Respect copyright: rewrite teacher-provided text only for their classroom use; for published works, summarize and quote short passages rather than reproducing them.

## For each level

- **The passage** — same key ideas, facts and vocabulary targets at every level so students can discuss together. Adjust: sentence length and complexity, paragraph length, amount of background explained, figurative language, and text structure signals (headings, "first / as a result").
  - Below grade: short sentences, one idea per sentence, key terms defined in context, headings and bolded vocabulary.
  - On grade: grade-typical sentence variety and vocabulary.
  - Above grade: more complex syntax, nuance, extra depth or a related extension idea.
- **Estimated reading level** (grade band), stated as an estimate.
- **Key vocabulary** (4-6 words): student-friendly definition, an example sentence, and for multilingual learners a cognate or visual cue where possible.

## Shared across levels

- **Text-dependent questions** — the same core questions for all levels (so the class can discuss together) plus one stretch question. Include an answer key with the evidence from the text.
- **Sentence frames** for answering in complete sentences.
- **Optional**: a short glossary in the students' home language(s), if requested; note that a fluent speaker should check it.

## Rules

- Don't change facts or water down the ideas — lower the language load, not the thinking.
- Label levels neutrally (e.g. "Version A / B / C" or colors) so students aren't labelled by reading level.

## Evidence base

Text complexity guidance (CCSS ELA Appendix A); UDL (CAST). Leveled texts are a bridge to grade-level text, not a replacement: also give students supported access to the grade-level version. Full citations and strength of evidence: `<plugin-root>/references/research.md`.
