## The daily routine (the same every day)

Students know this routine. Keeping it the same is the single most helpful thing you can do.

1. **Do Now (7 min):** problems are on the board when students walk in. Silent, independent. Go over 1-2 problems.
2. **Mini-lesson (13 min):** read the opening, then work through the examples on the board using the script. Ask students the question before each step, wait 3-5 seconds, then call on someone (not only volunteers).
3. **Guided practice (10 min):** do the first problem together; students do the rest with a partner.
4. **Independent practice (15 min):** students work alone; you circulate. Help students on the support list first.
5. **Exit ticket (5 min):** collect at the door. Sort into three piles (got it / nearly / not yet) and write the counts in the handoff log. Use the "not yet" pile to decide who to sit with tomorrow.

**If a lesson runs long:** cut independent practice, never the exit ticket. **If you finish early:** "find the mistake" problems, the next practice problems, or the challenge listed in the lesson.
