# Classroom Procedures — Room B12 (fictional sample)

## Arrival & attendance
- Greet students at the door. Bell-ringer is always posted on the left whiteboard.
- Take attendance in the online system within the first 10 minutes (login card is in the top desk drawer). If the system is down, use the paper roster in the red sub folder and send it to the front office.

## Emergency procedures
- **Fire:** Exit left, use the north stairwell, meet at the soccer field fence, section B. Take the red sub folder (it has the roster).
- **Lockdown:** Lock door, lights off, students to the back corner away from the window, silence. Do not open the door for anyone.
- **Medical:** Call the front office (ext. 100). Zoe F. may need her inhaler — it is with the nurse.

## Routines
- Bathroom: one student at a time, sign out on the clipboard by the door.
- Lab materials stay in the blue bins unless the plan says otherwise. No lab activities with a sub.
- Early finishers: science reading bin by the window.
- Dismissal: students stay seated until the bell; leave the room tidy.
