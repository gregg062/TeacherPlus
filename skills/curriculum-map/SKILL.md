# Curriculum map & pacing guide

## Ask once (only what's missing)

Course and grade(s), standards framework, school calendar (start/end dates, breaks, testing windows) or number of instructional days, required units/texts, common assessments, and whether this is for one teacher or a department.

## Produce

1. **Scope and sequence**: ordered units with a one-line rationale for the order (prerequisites first, building complexity, seasonal or testing considerations).
2. **Pacing calendar**: a table — **Unit | Dates / weeks | Days | Key standards | Big idea | Summative assessment**. Count real instructional days, leave ~10% buffer for reteaching, assemblies and testing, and put state testing prep where it belongs.
3. **Standards coverage check**: every required standard → the unit(s) where it's taught and assessed. Flag standards not covered and standards over-covered.
4. **Spiral / retrieval plan**: where key skills come back later in the year.
5. For departments: **common assessments** and when they occur, plus **vertical alignment** notes (what the previous grade should have covered and what the next grade expects).

Offer to expand any unit with `unit-planner`.

## Rules

- Be realistic: a map that doesn't fit the calendar is useless. Show the arithmetic for days.
- Mark any standard codes you're not certain of with "verify".
