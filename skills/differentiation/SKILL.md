# Differentiation & scaffolds

Differentiate **access**, not expectations: every student works toward the same learning goal, with different support or challenge.

## 1. Understand the task

Ask for the lesson/task and the goal if not provided. Ask which needs to plan for (or infer from the request): students who need support, multilingual learners, students with specific accommodations, advanced learners. No student names are needed — plan by need.

## 2. Produce what fits

- **Tiered versions** of the task (support / core / extension) that all hit the same objective. Show them side by side.
- **Scaffolds**: worked example then faded examples, step-by-step checklist, hint cards (in increasing levels of help), partially completed notes, a word bank, sentence frames, a model answer.
- **Graphic organizers**: pick the right one for the thinking (cause/effect, compare/contrast, sequence, claim-evidence-reasoning, concept map) and lay it out as a table or printable template.
- **Choice boards** (3x3 or menu): every option practices the same skill; vary the product (write, draw, explain, build, record) and include a "must do" square.
- **Extensions**: deeper, not more — transfer to a new context, create a question, evaluate a flawed answer, connect to another idea.

## 3. Accommodations → concrete changes

Translate each support into exactly what changes on the material or in the room, for example:
- *Read aloud* → audio or reading-partner plan; instructions read to the student; no reading penalty on non-reading tasks.
- *Reduced problem set* → mark the essential items (e.g. starred odd problems that still cover every skill).
- *Extended time* → plan when that time happens and what others do meanwhile.
- *Chunking* → break into sections with check-in points.
- *Multilingual learners* → visuals, bilingual glossary, sentence frames, partner in shared language, oral responses allowed.

Remind the teacher that legally required accommodations come from the student's IEP/504 plan and the case manager is the authority on them.
