## Introduction letter (send the Friday before the leave)

Dear Algebra 1 families,

I'm writing to let you know that I will be on leave from Monday, January 4, through Friday, January 29. I'll be back on Monday, February 1.

While I'm away, [Mr./Ms. Sub Name] will teach our class. [He/She/They] will follow detailed lesson plans I have prepared, so the class will continue exactly where we left off. We will be finishing our unit on systems of equations and inequalities, with quizzes on January 8, 15 and 22 and a unit test on January 29.

How you can help:
- Ask your student to show you one problem from that day's practice and explain each step.
- Encourage them to come to [help time, e.g. Tuesday/Thursday lunch in room 214] if they're stuck.

If you have questions about the class during my leave, please contact [Department Head Name] at [email]. Thank you for your support!

Sincerely,
[Ms. Rivera]
