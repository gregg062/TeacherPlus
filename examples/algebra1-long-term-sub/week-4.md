### Mini-lesson: worked examples (script)

**Worked example 1:** A chemistry teacher mixes a 10% salt solution with a 40% salt solution to get 30 liters of a 20% solution. How many liters of each?

Write each step on the board and say what you're doing and why. Ask the question in italics before writing the step and give 3-5 seconds of think time.

1. Define the variables: x = liters of 10% solution, y = liters of 40% solution
2. Write the system: x + y = 30  and  0.1x + 0.4y = 6
3. Line up the equations in standard form:  (1) x + y = 30   (2) 0.1x + 0.4y = 6
4. Clear the decimals in equation (2): multiply every term by 10: x + 4y = 60
5. Multiply EVERY term of equation (2) by −1: −x − 4y = −60
6. Add the equations. The x terms cancel: −3y = −30
7. Divide both sides by −3: y = 10
8. Substitute y = 10 into equation (1): x + (10) = 30 → x = 20
9. Solution (20, 10). Check in equation (2): (20) + 4(10) = 60 ✓
10. Answer the question in a sentence: Use 20 liters of the 10% solution and 10 liters of the 40% solution.

Say: *"Use 20 liters of the 10% solution and 10 liters of the 40% solution. How do we know it's right?"* (We checked it in the original equations.)

**Worked example 2:** A kayak goes 24 miles downstream in 2 hours and returns upstream in 3 hours. Find the kayak's speed in still water and the current's speed.

Write each step on the board and say what you're doing and why. Ask the question in italics before writing the step and give 3-5 seconds of think time.

   - Divide both sides by 6: x = 3

**Do Now 2.** Solve by substitution:  y = 3x + 2  and  3x − y = −2.  
**Answer: infinitely many solutions**
   - The first equation already says y = 3x + 2.
   - Substitute (3x + 2) for y in the second equation, keeping the parentheses: 3x − (3x + 2) = −2
   - Distribute: 3x − 3x − 2 = −2
   - Combine like terms: the x terms cancel, leaving −2 = −2
   - −2 = −2 is always true, so every point on the line works: infinitely many solutions (the equations are the same line).
