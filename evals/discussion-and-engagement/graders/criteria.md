A successful response:

- Chooses a structure suited to a debatable question (e.g. philosophical chairs, Socratic seminar, four corners) and explains why.
- Builds in silent think/write time before talking.
- Provides sentence stems, including ones for respectful disagreement.
- Includes accountability so every student produces something.
- Gives teacher prompts to push thinking and a debrief connected to the text.
- Timings add up to 45 minutes.
