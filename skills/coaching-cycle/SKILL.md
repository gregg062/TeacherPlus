# Coaching cycle

A typical cycle is **4-6 weeks**: identify a goal → learn the strategy → practice → implement and observe → reflect on student results.

## 1. Goal-setting conversation

Prepare questions to help the teacher set a goal grounded in **student** learning or behavior, e.g. "What do you want students to be able to do that they can't yet?" Then shape it as: *By [date], [students] will [measurable change], as shown by [evidence], because I will [teaching strategy].*

## 2. Cycle plan

A table: **Week | Coach does | Teacher does | Evidence collected**. Include:
- a baseline measure of the student outcome (exit tickets, participation tallies, work samples);
- the strategy explained and modeled (coach models in class, video, or co-plans);
- rehearsal/practice before trying it with students;
- 2-3 short observations focused only on the goal, with the look-fors;
- data checkpoints and a final measure.

## 3. Debrief conversations

For each debrief: opening question, a look at the evidence together, reflective questions ("What did you notice when...?"), one next step, and when to check back. Use questions before suggestions; offer direct guidance when the teacher asks or is new.

## 4. Cycle summary

One page: goal, baseline vs. final evidence, what the teacher did, what changed for students, what's next. Celebrate progress.

## Rules

- Coaching is non-evaluative unless the school says otherwise; keep coaching notes separate from evaluations.
- Keep the focus on one goal per cycle.
