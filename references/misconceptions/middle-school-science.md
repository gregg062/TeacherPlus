# Middle school science — common misconceptions

Status: DRAFT, awaiting educator review. Many are well documented in the research literature on students' alternative conceptions; see e.g. Driver et al., *Making Sense of Secondary Science* (1994).

## Life science
- **Plants get their food (mass) from the soil.** Response: Van Helmont's willow tree experiment; most plant mass comes from carbon dioxide in the air via photosynthesis.
- **Plants don't respire / only animals use oxygen.** Response: plants carry out cellular respiration all the time; photosynthesis only in light.
- **Evolution means individuals change during their lifetime because they need to.** Response: variation already exists; selection acts on populations over generations.
- **Bigger organisms have bigger cells.** Response: compare cell sizes; more cells, not bigger cells.
- **Energy is recycled in ecosystems like matter.** Response: matter cycles, energy flows and is lost as heat at each level (10% rule as an approximation).

## Physical science
- **Heavier objects fall faster.** Response: drop tests with equal-shape objects of different mass; air resistance is the confounder.
- **A moving object needs a force to keep moving.** Response: friction is the hidden force; air-hockey/ice examples; Newton's first law.
- **Heat and temperature are the same thing.** Response: a bathtub of warm water vs. a cup of boiling water.
- **Cold flows into things.** Response: thermal energy moves from warmer to cooler.
- **Mass is lost when something dissolves or burns.** Response: closed-system mass measurements on a scale (sugar dissolving in water; baking soda and vinegar in a bottle sealed with a balloon). Follow school lab safety rules for any reaction.
- **Atoms in a solid don't move; atoms are alive / can be seen.** Response: particle model animations; particles always move.

## Earth and space science
- **Seasons are caused by distance from the sun.** Response: Earth is closest to the sun in January; it's the tilt (and the hemispheres have opposite seasons).
- **The moon's phases are caused by Earth's shadow.** Response: lamp-and-ball model; that is a lunar eclipse.
- **Rocks don't change / the Earth's surface is fixed.** Response: rock cycle, plate movement in cm/year (fingernail growth).
- **Weather and climate are the same.** Response: weather is the day's conditions; climate is the long-term pattern.

## Nature of science
- **A theory is just a guess.** Response: a scientific theory is a well-tested explanation supported by much evidence.
- **Experiments prove things true.** Response: evidence supports or refutes; science is revised with new evidence.
