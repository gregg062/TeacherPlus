A successful response:

- Opens warmly with a specific positive (class discussions).
- States the concern factually (4 missing assignments, 2 weeks).
- Says what the teacher is doing and gives a concrete ask or option.
- Invites the parent to share what would help.
- Under about 200 words, no jargon; presented as a draft, not sent.
