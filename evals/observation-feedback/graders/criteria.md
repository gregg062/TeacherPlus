A successful response:

- Leads with evidence-based praise (rapport, posted objective).
- Uses low-inference evidence rather than judgments.
- Gives ONE clear next step (e.g. replace 'any questions?' with all-student checks/cold call after think time).
- Includes a reflective question and a practice plan.
- Respectful, growth-focused tone; under about 300 words for the main feedback.
