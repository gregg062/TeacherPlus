## Partial credit (quizzes and test)

Each "solve the system" question is worth 4 points:
- **2 points: method.** The setup is correct (substitution with parentheses, elimination with the whole equation multiplied, a correct graph).
- **1 point: correct values.** Both x and y are correct.
- **1 point: check / ordered pair.** Written as an ordered pair (x, y), with a check shown.
- A small arithmetic slip with correct method: 3 of 4.
- "No solution" or "infinitely many" questions: 2 for the algebra ending in a false or true statement, 2 for the correct conclusion.

For graphing inequalities: 1 point boundary line, 1 point solid/dashed correct, 2 points correct shading.
